Your finances never leave your device

SpendWise stores everything in localStorage on the machine you're using. There is no database, no analytics, no tracking and no account sync.

  • One network call. Live exchange rates from open.er-api.com, cached for 24 hours. Block it and the app still works at 1:1.
  • No passwords stored. Sign-in is a local profile so the app can greet you. Nothing is hashed, stored or transmitted.
  • Clear it any time. Settings → Clear all data, or clear your browser storage. There is nothing else to delete.
